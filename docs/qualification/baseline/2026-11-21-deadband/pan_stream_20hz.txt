shoulder_pan following a 20 Hz stream, arm 1 (so100_cell), 2026-11-21.

Method: a probe node inside so100-control publishes /arm1/joint_targets at ~20 Hz for 8 s with
pan = 16.835 + 5*(1-cos(2*pi*t/8)), i.e. 0 -> 10 -> 0 deg and back; every second it reads /read on
8002 and records commanded vs actual. Torque on for the run, off afterwards.

150 messages, 18.8 Hz, worst error 1.276 deg.

What it says: the stream is accepted and followed; the error changes sign with the direction, which is lag
(the arm is behind the ramp), not the deadband — a deadband would show as a step that never closes, and
these all close as the ramp turns around. 1.3 deg of lag at 0.6 deg/s of commanded motion is the number a
policy has to live with when it streams, and it is why a closed loop needs to look at the reading and not at
what it sent.

What it does not say: the lag at higher rates or larger amplitudes (not measured), and whether arm 2 behaves
the same (not measured; its pan undershoots more at every step).

Two things this adds. The pan peaks agree with the readings taken inside the container (1.912 and 2.379
here, 1.912 and 2.546 there) — two independent paths, same order, so the twin is not inventing numbers.
And wrist_flex sits at exactly -0.704 deg on every line and never closes: that is the deadband in the wild,
a command the servo simply does not act on, visible for the first time as a number that refuses to move
while the pan errors open and close with the ramp.
