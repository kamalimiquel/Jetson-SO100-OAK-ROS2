Deadband of shoulder_pan, arm 2 (so100_cell_b), 2026-11-21.

Method: the same one used on arm 1, unchanged: torque on, one step per publication to /arm2/joint_targets
(six values, jaw held), settle 3 s, read /read on the arm's own port. Each target is the last reading plus
the step, so the steps are consecutive and the arm walks forward. Torque off at the end.

Result: 0.5 deg moved nothing, twice; 1.0 deg moved nothing; 2.0 deg moved 1.495; 5.0 deg moved 4.308.
The threshold therefore lies between 1.0 and 2.0 deg, double arm 1's, and every step overshoots on the
short side — 0.505 and 0.692 deg of error where arm 1 hit 5.011 of 5.

What this rules out: that the deadband is a property of the model. Same model, same firmware, same
procedure, twice the threshold; and the two arms do not share their scales or their jaw curves either.

What it does not say: whether the other five joints of this arm behave the same, whether the threshold or
the undershoot grows under load, and whether the extra friction is the servo or the printed joint. A 1 deg
step is ~11 counts of the 4096-per-turn encoder, so the servo is being asked clearly and not moving: it is a
deadband, not quantisation, on this arm too.
