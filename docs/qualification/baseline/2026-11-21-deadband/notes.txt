Deadband of shoulder_pan, arm 1 (so100_cell), 2026-11-21.

Method: torque on, one step per publication to /arm1/joint_targets (six values, jaw held), settle 3 s,
read /read. Each target is the last reading plus the step, so the steps are consecutive and the arm walks
forward. Torque off at the end.

Result: 0.5 deg produces no movement, twice, from the same starting pose. 1.0 deg moves (1.231 achieved),
2.0 deg moves 1.846, 5.0 deg moves 5.011. The threshold therefore lies between 0.5 and 1.0 deg.

What this rules out: that the earlier 1 deg step which "did not move" was a protocol error. It was below
or at the threshold in that direction from that pose; the contract accepted the write, the servo did not
move. Anything a policy commands below ~1 deg on this joint is a request the hardware may silently ignore.

What it does not say: whether the threshold is the same on the other five joints (not measured), whether it
is a servo deadband or stiction in the printed joint, and whether it changes with load (not measured).
Tick resolution is 4096 per turn = 0.088 deg, so a 0.5 deg step is ~5.7 ticks and is still ignored: this is
a deadband, not quantisation.
