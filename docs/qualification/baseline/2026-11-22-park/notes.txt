Parked pose, both arms, measured 2026-11-22.

Method: torque off on both arms, folded by hand to a pose clear of the table, the other arm and the cables;
then /read on each arm's own port. No motion commanded.

Result (degrees, as reported): see parked_pose.csv. arm 1 wrist_roll -108.0 and arm 2 -87.1 differ by 21 deg;
the pan differs by 4.8 deg. The pose that was in the code before this (lift -95, wrist_flex 60, roll 0) put
wrist_flex 20 deg and wrist_roll 87-108 deg away from where the arms actually park, so a folded arm read as
"not parked" and the supervisor would have refused every mission.

What this rules out: that one pose can serve both arms. It cannot: one number would fail one of them.

What it does not say: the repeatability of the fold. This is ONE fold per arm; the tolerance is 6 deg, three
times arm 2's measured pan deadband, which covers the deadband but has not been checked against the spread of
folding by hand. Fold each arm twice more and compare before trusting the tolerance for autonomy.
