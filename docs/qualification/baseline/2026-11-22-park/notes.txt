Parked pose, both arms, measured 2026-11-22.

Method: torque off on both arms, folded by hand to a pose clear of the table, the other arm and the cables;
then /read on each arm's own port. No motion commanded.

Result (degrees, as reported): see parked_pose.csv. arm 1 wrist_roll -108.0 and arm 2 -87.1 differ by 21 deg;
the pan differs by 4.8 deg. The pose that was in the code before this (lift -95, wrist_flex 60, roll 0) put
wrist_flex 20 deg and wrist_roll 87-108 deg away from where the arms actually park, so a folded arm read as
"not parked" and the supervisor would have refused every mission.

What this rules out: that one pose can serve both arms. It cannot: one number would fail one of them.

What it does not say: the repeatability of the fold. This is ONE fold per arm; the tolerance is 6 deg, three
times arm 2's measured pan deadband, which covers the deadband but has not been checked against the spread of
folding by hand. Fold each arm twice more and compare before trusting the tolerance for autonomy.

PARK AS A SKILL — spread, 2026-11-22.
Commanded twice per arm from the folded pose, 2.5 s each, read after each. Spread is 0.00 deg on every joint of
both arms: a commanded park is repeatable, unlike a fold by hand (never measured twice).

But wrist_roll lands 14.2 deg (arm 1) and 17.8 deg (arm 2) away from the pose that was defined, while every
other joint lands within 0.8 deg. Two candidate explanations, neither proved:
  a) a units round trip: the pose was measured with /read and written back as sdk, and something between the
     write and the reading scales it by ~1/0.877 (arm 1) and ~1/0.825 (arm 2) — both match that joint's
     measured roll scale, which is suspicious;
  b) the roll servo does not hold that angle under torque, or the cable limits it.
(a) is more likely: the discrepancy tracks each arm's own roll scale.
Until this is explained, the park skill must not be trusted for autonomy: park.check_arm compares /read with
PARKED, so a commanded park currently reads as "not parked" — which is the guard working, not a bug in the guard.
Next measurement: write a single +1 deg step to wrist_roll and read it back, as the deadband test did for the pan.

CORRECTION, same day. The two rows previously marked UNEXPLAINED (wrist_roll 14 and 18 deg off) were a fault
in my probe script, not in the arm or in the skill: the script converted the pose with joint_scale before
writing, so wrist_roll was sent to -123 instead of -108. Driving the pose through skill_plan and writing what it
returns puts every joint within 0.47 deg, wrist_roll included (arm 1: -108.00 -> -108.09).

Check that ruled the conversion out: a 10 deg step on wrist_roll comes back as 0.976 of the step on both arms
(0.703 and 0.791 for a 1 deg step, which is the deadband, not a scale). A units round trip would have returned
0.877 and 0.825 — the two roll scales. It did not.

So: the park skill is correct, the parked pose is reachable under torque to within half a degree, and the
spread of two commanded parks is 0.00 deg. What is still unmeasured is the spread of folding BY HAND, which the
skill makes unnecessary.
